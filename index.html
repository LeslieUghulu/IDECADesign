The Ivory Reverie Gown is a timeless expression of femininity and grace. Our Story

About ideca

Miranda Ughulu

The Designer

Miranda Ughulu

ideca Designs Inc. was born from a lifelong devotion to the art of fashion. Founded by Miranda Ughulu, ideca is a fashion design and clothing establishment that exists at the intersection of elegance and boldness — where every piece tells a story.

Inspired by the timeless sophistication of Badgley Mischka, the refined modernity of Michael Kors, and the dramatic romance of Valentino, Miranda has carved a distinct identity in the world of fashion — one that speaks directly to the woman who is unapologetically herself.

Every collection — from ready-to-wear to fully custom designs — is created with an unwavering commitment to craftsmanship, quality, and the belief that every woman deserves to feel extraordinary.

— Miranda Ughulu, Founder & Creative Director
